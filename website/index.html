Blue-light & flicker filter for macOS

Your Mac,
warmer and steadier.

EyeEase warms your screen past where Night Shift stops, and holds the backlight at 100% so it can't flicker while you dim. Two sliders, three presets, and one switch that takes blue to zero.

Drag it. This runs the same blackbody maths the app uses — at the far end, the blue channel is zero.

01

Zero blue, zero flicker

One switch. The blue channel is written as literal zeros, not merely turned down, and the backlight is pinned so nothing strobes while the dimming happens in software.

02

Warms itself on a schedule

Fixed hours, or real sunset and sunrise from your coordinates — worked out on your machine with a bit of astronomy, not fetched from anyone.

03

Never touches the network

No accounts, no analytics, no update pings. There is not a single network call in the source, which you are welcome to go and check.
